# Ongea Pesa — Brand Foundation

The single source of truth for the launch. Everything in this kit derives from this document.

---

## 1. Brand strategy

| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Voice-first mobile money / fintech PWA (Kenya). |
| **Audience** | Everyday Kenyans — M-Pesa generation, hustlers, chama members, small traders. Mobile-only, Swahili/Sheng-fluent, value speed and trust over UI polish. |
| **Product function** | You *speak* a payment ("Tuma elfu mbili kwa Mama Boi") and it's sent. Voice → intent → money moves. |
| **Emotional promise** | Effortless control of money. No menus, no typing, no fear. "It just heard me and did it." |
| **Cultural position** | Proudly Nairobi-built. Modern African fintech that feels global-premium but locally fluent. |
| **Trust level** | High-stakes (real money). Must feel **secure, precise, alive** — never gimmicky. |
| **Core metaphor** | **Voice as energy.** A spoken word becomes a pulse of light that orbits and resolves into a transaction. Sound = the orbiting waveform; intelligence = the glowing core; money = the motion. |
| **What to avoid** | Generic purple-blue "AI glow", clip-art microphones, dollar signs, busy gradients, Western-bank stiffness, cheap neon. |

**Positioning line:** *The bank that listens.*
**Tagline (primary):** **Speak. Send. Done.**
**Tagline (Swahili):** **Ongea. Pesa itembee.** ("Speak. Let money move.")

## 2. Logo — the mark

The existing app icon is the genesis: a luminous **orbital core** (the intelligence) wrapped in **soundwave rings** (the voice), reading as a small glowing planet of sound. We *refine and elevate* it — same DNA, studio-grade execution.

**Symbolic logic (keep across every variation):**
- **Core** = the listening intelligence (bright teal-cyan center).
- **Orbiting waveform** = the spoken command in motion.
- **Ring/orbit** = money completing its journey, a closed loop = "done / settled".
- Reads as: a voice becoming a transaction.

**Construction rules:**
- Built on a circle; orbit paths at consistent angles; waveform amplitude symmetric.
- Icon must survive at 16px (favicon) — at small sizes, drop fine particles, keep core + 2 wave arcs + 1 orbit.
- Always has breathing room: clear space = height of the "O" in the wordmark on all sides.

## 3. Color system — "Voice Teal"

Ongea Pesa owns **teal → cyan → signal-green** (voice-tech, alive, trustworthy). See `brand-tokens.json` for machine-readable values.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Voice Teal** | `#0FB5A6` | Primary brand color. Core of the mark, primary buttons. |
| **Electric Cyan** | `#22D3EE` | Highlight / glow / soundwave peaks. |
| **Signal Green** | `#34D399` | Success, "money moved", positive accents. |
| **Abyss Ink** | `#0A1A1F` | Primary dark canvas (near-black teal-tinted). |
| **Deep Sea** | `#0E2A30` | Secondary dark panel. |
| **Mist** | `#E6F7F5` | Light tint background. |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Light canvas, wordmark on dark. |
| **Halo Glow** | `radial #22D3EE → transparent` | The signature luminous bloom behind the mark. |

**Gradient (signature):** `#34D399 → #0FB5A6 → #22D3EE` along the orbit (green resolving into cyan = "money settling into signal").

**Accent discipline:** teal family carries 90% of the system. Signal Green only for success/positive. No other hues introduced.

## 4. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Wordmark / logo** | **Sora** (or Space Grotesk) — geometric, slightly futuristic, tight tracking, uppercase **ONGEA PESA**. | Modern African-tech, confident, voice-forward. |
| **Headlines** | **Sora** Semibold / Bold. | Pairs with wordmark, high hierarchy. |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter**. | Neutral, hyper-legible on mobile, Swahili diacritics safe. |
| **Mono / data** | **JetBrains Mono** (amounts, codes, "KES 2,000"). | Precision, fintech trust. |

**Wordmark spec:** uppercase, letter-spacing +6%, "ONGEA" and "PESA" equal weight, optical centering under the mark. Never condense, never outline, never add drop-shadow except the brand halo glow.

**Custom merch display font:** for apparel, a bespoke wide-stretched uppercase treatment of "ONGEA PESA" (see `02_Apparel`). Tall, athletic, slightly extended — reads from across a room.

## 6. Voice & tone

- **Confident, warm, local.** Speaks Sheng-fluent English. ("Sema. Tuma. Done.")
- Short. Imperative. Like the product: you speak, it acts.
- Never corporate-stiff, never over-explaining, never patronizing.

**Do say:** "Speak. Send. Done." · "The bank that listens." · "Pesa, kwa sauti yako." (Money, by your voice.)
**Don't say:** "Revolutionary AI-powered financial ecosystem solution."

---

## 7. Anti-slop guardrails (applies to every generated image)

- No generic purple-blue AI gradient for Ongea (that's reserved for NSAIT endorser only).
- No floating clip-art icons, no literal dollar signs, no stock people holding phones.
- No messy moodboard collages, no fake tiny lorem text.
- One dominant teal palette. One clear idea per image. Generous negative space.
- Every render must look like it came from a serious Nairobi design studio — premium, intentional, ownable.
