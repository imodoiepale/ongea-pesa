# Apparel — Print-Ready Specs

Hand these to your embroiderer / DTG / screen printer.

## Left-breast lockup (front, both garments)
- **Width:** 80–90 mm (3.1–3.5 in) max. Emblem ~28 mm tall.
- **Position:** 175–190 mm down from the high point of the shoulder seam, ~100 mm right of center (wearer's left chest).
- **NSAIT mark:** sits 6 mm below the wordmark, max 40 mm wide.
- **Method:** embroidery (use `01_Logo_System/prompts/04_monochrome.json` master for stitch) OR DTG full-color for the gradient emblem.
- **Thread match:** Teal ≈ Madeira Polyneon 1949 / Pantone 3275 C; Cyan ≈ Pantone 3115 C; Green ≈ Pantone 3395 C.

## Back print (large)
- **Width:** 280–320 mm (11–12.5 in) across the shoulders.
- **Position:** top of print 80–100 mm below collar seam.
- **Content:** emblem + **ONGEA PESA** (unique display font) + line "by Nairobi Space of AI Tools · NSAIT".
- **Method:** screen print (spot colors, best for the wide flat wordmark) or DTG for gradient.
- **Underline soundwave:** thin, 280 mm wide, signature gradient `#34D399 → #22D3EE`.

## File deliverables for printer
- Vector **SVG/PDF** of each lockup (emblem + wordmark), outlined fonts.
- **CMYK** + **Pantone** spec sheet (spot colors above).
- Transparent **PNG @ 300 dpi** at final print size for DTG.
- Mono 1-color version for embroidery digitizing.

- **Polo:** 200–220 gsm pique cotton, side-vented, ribbed collar.
