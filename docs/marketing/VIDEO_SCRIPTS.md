# Ongea Pesa — 10 Viral Video Scripts

> **Format:** Vertical 9:16 · 15–30 seconds · Hook-first · Premium, aspirational, convenience-led
> **For:** Higgsfield storyboarding → video generation → TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
> **Brand:** Voice-first, hands-free, premium Kenyan fintech. Tagline: *"Speak. Done."*

## Script 1 — THE VOICE PAY HERO
**"She paid without looking at her phone"**

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Hook (0–2s)** | On-screen text: *"She paid without touching her phone"* · Tight shot of a woman's face — composed, eyes forward |
| **Beat 1 (2–6s)** | She's at a restaurant. Waiter brings the bill. She glances at it briefly, then looks back at her conversation partner. Lifts her phone slightly. |
| **Beat 2 (6–10s)** | Mouth close-up: *"Lipa bili"* ("Pay the bill"). The phone screen glows emerald — animated waveform pulses. |
| **Beat 3 (10–16s)** | The Ongea Pesa slot panel on screen fills: **KSh 3,200 · Mulla's Kitchen · Paybill** — each field ticks in with a green check. |
| **Beat 4 (16–20s)** | Agent voice (warm, confident): *"Paid. Mulla's Kitchen. Three thousand, two hundred shillings."* |
| **Beat 5 (20–24s)** | She sets the phone face-down and raises her wine glass. The waiter gives a small nod. |
| **CTA (24–28s)** | Text overlay: *"Ongea Pesa. Speak. Done."* · Logo fade-in on charcoal background |
| **VO** | "When money moves at the speed of your voice, life keeps moving with you." |
| **Higgsfield prompt** | Elegant rooftop restaurant, Nairobi night skyline, cinematic 4K, shallow DOF, emerald app glow, warm candlelight, slow-motion pour, premium luxury feel |

## Script 3 — THE FAMILY TRANSFER
**"Two thousand shillings. One word."**

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Hook (0–3s)** | Split screen. Left: busy Nairobi office. Right: rural home, warm afternoon. Text: *"1,000 km apart. 3 seconds apart."* |
| **Beat 1 (3–8s)** | Left: man at his desk, phone in hand. He speaks softly: *"Tuma mama elfu mbili."* ("Send mom two thousand.") |
| **Beat 2 (8–14s)** | On his screen: the voice panel fills — *KSh 2,000 · Mama Wanjiku · Send Money* — each slot ticks. Agent: *"Sent. Your mother received two thousand shillings."* |
| **Beat 3 (14–20s)** | Right (rural home): the mother's old phone buzzes. She picks it up. M-Pesa notification: *+KSh 2,000*. Her face transforms. She clasps her hands. A small laugh escapes her. |
| **Beat 4 (20–25s)** | The son smiles at his desk. Puts the phone down. Back to work. |
| **CTA (25–30s)** | Text: *"Free transfers to every Ongea user."* · Logo |
| **VO** | "The people you love shouldn't feel far away. One word. Sent." |
| **Higgsfield prompt** | Emotional dual-location composition, Nairobi office blue tones + rural Kenya warm amber, cinematic 4K, authentic Kenyan faces, genuine emotion |
