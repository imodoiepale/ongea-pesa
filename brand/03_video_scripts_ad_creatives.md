## SCRIPT 1 — "The Blindfold Challenge" (TikTok/Reels, 30–45s) — FLAGSHIP

**Format:** split screen or two-take. Creator + timer overlay.

- HOOK (0–3s): Creator ties blindfold. "Sending 500 bob to mum. Blindfolded. First: the normal way."
- (3–15s): Fumbling through menus, mis-taps, frustration. Timer running. "Wapi send money... is this it? I just called my landlord."
- (15–20s): Rip blindfold moment — "Now watch this." Re-ties blindfold. Opens Ongea Pesa.
- (20–30s): "Tuma mia tano kwa mum." App voice: "Kutuma KSh 500 kwa Mum. Sema ndiyo kuconfirm." — "Ndiyo." — Chime. Timer stops: 8 seconds.
- CTA: "Now imagine you had no blindfold to take off. Ongea Pesa. Sema tu." #BlindfoldChallenge #OngeaPesa

## SCRIPT 2 — "Amina" (Documentary short, 90s, YouTube + cutdowns)

**Cast a real visually impaired user (paid, credited, consulted on the edit).**

- (0–10s) B-roll: Nairobi morning, sounds foregrounded. VO (Amina): "People think the hardest thing about being blind is crossing the road. It's not."
- (10–25s) Reenactment: Amina at a shop, phone handed over. "It's handing your phone to a stranger and saying your PIN out loud. Everyone in the queue knows your balance."
- (25–40s) "My money was never private. My money was never mine."
- (40–70s) Turn: Amina alone, at home. "Tuma elfu mbili kwa Baraka." Confirmation chime. She smiles. "The first time I sent money completely alone, I was 34 years old."
- (70–90s) "Ongea Pesa doesn't see my eyes. It hears me. And it answers." — TITLE CARD: *Independence you can hear. Ongea Pesa.*

## SCRIPT 3 — "PIN Yako Ni Siri?" (street interviews, 60s)

Vox pops in town: "Nani anajua PIN yako ya M-Pesa?" Count the answers on screen ("My sister... my shopkeeper... my son"). Beat drop: "Sasa — who knows your *voice*? Nobody can borrow it. Nobody can guess it." Demo clip. CTA.

## SCRIPT 4 — "Chama Treasurer" (skit, 45s)

Stressed treasurer drowning in screenshots and "nimetuma" lies. She says: "Ongea Pesa, anza collection ya chama — kila mtu elfu moja." Phone: "STK zimetumwa kwa wanachama 12." Members' phones ping around the table. "Nani hajalipa?" — "Kevin." Everyone stares at Kevin. Comedy + product truth.

## SCRIPT 5 — "The Twin Test" (security demo, 60s)

Real twins. Twin A registers. Twin B tries everything — same face shape, similar voice — to move Twin A's money. Fails. Then a laptop plays a cloned AI voice. Fails (liveness detection). "Your voice has a fingerprint. We check it every time." CTA.
