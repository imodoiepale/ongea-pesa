# 05 · Nairobi Content Shoot Locations & Permits

## A. Permits & costs (Kenya Film Commission / KFCB, 2026)

- Short content/documentary filming license: ~KES 5,000 (USD 50); full feature/TV series ~KES 15,000. Daily filming fee ~KES 1,000/day. Short-production permits are processed in minutes–hours via KFC.
- **Nairobi CBD / city streets:** separate county permit (~KES 3,640/day photography; street closures need 2+ weeks notice).
- Phone-shot social content in public spaces is commonly done permit-free in practice, but for anything with a crew/tripod/talent, get the license — we are a fintech seeking a CBK license; be squeaky clean.
- Private venues (malls, cafés, offices): venue's written permission replaces county hassle. Fastest route for skits.

## B. Location list by script (see doc 03)

### Blindfold Challenge / Sema Tu skits (fast, cheap)
- **Java House / Artcaffe branch** (ask manager, off-peak) — recognizable middle-class Kenya.
- **Nairobi Garage or iHub coworking** — tech-credible backdrop, friendly to startups, indoor audio control.
- **A friend's apartment, Kilimani/South B** — zero cost, controlled sound.

### "Amina" documentary
- **Kenya Society for the Blind compound (Likoni Road)** — authentic, partner-endorsed; ask KSB during partnership talks.
- **Local duka/shopping corridor in Umoja or Kawangware** — the PIN-handover reenactment; get shopkeeper's consent + small facilitation fee.
- **User's own home** — the emotional core scene; natural light, lav mics.

### Street interviews ("PIN Yako Ni Siri?")
- **Kencom / Archives area, CBD** — the classic vox-pop spot (county permit needed).
- **Alternative without CBD permit: The Waterfront Karen, Two Rivers, Garden City** (mall permission) or **Jeevanjee Gardens**.

### Brand/hero visuals (premium look)
- **KICC rooftop/helipad** — the Nairobi skyline shot; book via KICC, ~KES 20–50K depending on use. The "built in Nairobi, for the world" money shot.
- **Nairobi National Park edge (Ivory Burning Site viewpoint)** — skyline + wildlife juxtaposition; KWS filming rates apply. Perfect for investor-deck B-roll: "only in Kenya."
- **Uhuru Park / Green Park terminus** — refreshed public space, free-ish, city energy.
- **BRT/Green Park matatu terminus + a branded matatu** — for the SACCO pilot campaign.

### Radio & sound
- **Homeboyz Radio / Radio Africa studios** — record radio spots and capture BTS content in one visit.

## C. Shoot logistics checklist

- [ ] KFC filming license (apply via kenyafilmcommission.go.ke; carry printout)
- [ ] County permit if CBD/street; venue letters otherwise
- [ ] Talent releases signed (bilingual EN/SW) — REQUIRED for all faces, especially minors: none without guardian consent
- [ ] Disabled talent: paid, consulted, edit-veto honored (production rule, doc 03)
- [ ] Lav mics ×2 + deadcat (we're a voice brand — bad audio is brand damage)
- [ ] Founder shirt packed + steamed; backup shirt
- [ ] Shot list: vertical master + horizontal safety for each setup
- [ ] Data wrangle: two SD cards, cloud upload same night
- [ ] Accessibility post: captions + audio-description track budgeted per video
