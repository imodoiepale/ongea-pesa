# 01 · Ongea Pesa Brand Bible

## 1. What we are (one breath)

**Ongea Pesa** ("Talk Money" in Swahili) is a voice-first fintech PWA that lets anyone — including the 970,000+ blind and visually impaired Kenyans — send, receive, and manage money by simply speaking. No screens to squint at, no menus to memorize, no stranger reading your balance out loud.

## 2. Mission, vision, promise

- **Mission:** Make money as easy as conversation, for everyone.
- **Vision:** A world where financial independence never depends on eyesight, literacy, or someone else's hands.
- **Brand promise:** *If you can speak, you can bank.*

## 3. Positioning statement

For Kenyans who find screens a barrier — the visually impaired, the elderly, the busy, the low-literacy user — Ongea Pesa is the voice-first money app that executes payments through natural conversation, secured by your own voice and face. Unlike M-Pesa's menus and sighted-helper workarounds, Ongea Pesa needs no eyes and no middleman.

## 4. Taglines (pick one primary, keep the rest for campaigns)

- **Primary: "Ongea. Tuma. Done."** (Speak. Send. Done.)
- "Your voice is your bank."
- "Pesa kwa sauti yako." (Money in your voice.)
- "Banking without barriers."
- "Sema tu." (Just say it.)
- Accessibility campaign: "Independence you can hear."

## 5. Brand personality & voice

**Personality:** A trusted friend who happens to be brilliant with money. Warm, direct, Kenyan, proud, never condescending — especially never pitying toward disabled users. We celebrate independence, we don't dramatize helplessness.

**Voice rules:**

- Sheng and Swahili are welcome and encouraged; code-switch naturally like Nairobi does.
- Say "blind and visually impaired users," never "the blind," never "victims" or "sufferers."
- Show ability, not charity. The hero of every story is the user, not the app.
- Short sentences. Spoken rhythm. If a line sounds wrong read aloud, rewrite it — we are a voice company.

## 6. Founder profile — James Epale

**James Epale** — Founder & CEO, Ongea Pesa (a product of **Nairobi Space of AI Tools / NSAIT**).

Bio (use verbatim in press kits, ~80 words):

Short bio (25 words): *James Epale is the Nairobi-based founder of Ongea Pesa, the voice-first payments app making money accessible to blind and visually impaired Africans.*

Founder story beats (for interviews — memorize these three):

1. **The moment:** a blind M-Pesa user dictating their PIN to a shopkeeper. Total strangers holding total financial control.
2. **The insight:** voice AI got good enough that speech is now more secure than a PIN a stranger can hear.
3. **The ambition:** Kenya invented mobile money for the world (M-Pesa). Kenya can invent *accessible* money for the world.

## 7. Visual identity (working spec — refine with a designer)

- **Colors:** Deep green (money/growth, Kenyan identity) + warm gold (value) + white. High-contrast pairs only — WCAG AAA is a brand requirement, not a nice-to-have.
- **Type:** Big, bold, humanist sans-serif. Minimum 18px equivalent in all materials. If a sighted 70-year-old can't read it at arm's length, it fails.
- **Sound logo:** We are a voice brand — commission a 1.5-second audio mark (a two-note "O-P" motif) used at the start of every video and in-app confirmation. This is our most important logo.
- **Imagery:** Real Nairobi, real hands, real faces. Never stock-photo pity shots of disabled people. Users mid-laugh, mid-transaction, mid-life.

## 10. Message hierarchy (what we say, in order)

1. **Independence** — "No one should need another person's eyes to use their own money."
2. **Simplicity** — "Sema tu." It's faster to say it than to type it.
3. **Security** — Your voice + your face = keys nobody can steal. (Never lead with security; lead with freedom, prove with security.)
4. **Kenyan pride** — Built in Nairobi, for the world. M-Pesa's true heir.
